Hourglass Randomify

What this tool does

Hourglass Randomify is a visual way to plan and log your time instead of typing entries one by one directly in Clockify.

  • Drag a project or task onto a day and pick roughly how long it took — the tool turns that into a real Clockify time entry.
  • Switch to Shares mode when you don't remember exact hours, only roughly how your day was split — it's always converted to real hours before anything is sent.
  • Log a whole week or month as a single bar instead of day by day, and the tool splits the total evenly across your working days (Monday–Friday).
  • Review what you've logged in the History tab.

Don't forget to save

Nothing is kept automatically — your logged hours, API key, and favorites only persist once you click Save, in the top-right corner of the tool.

Getting your Clockify API key

This tool needs a personal Clockify API key to read and write time entries on your behalf. Here's how to get one:

  1. 1

    In Clockify, click your profile avatar in the top-right corner and choose Preferences.

  2. 2

    On the Preferences page, open the Advanced tab and click Manage API keys.

  3. 3

    On the Manage API keys page, click Generate new.

  4. 4

    Give the key a name you'll recognize later — e.g. "Day splitter" — and click Generate.

  5. 5

    Clockify shows you the key once — copy it now. You won't be able to view it again after closing this dialog.

  6. 6

    Come back to this tool, open its Settings tab (the gear icon), paste the key into the API key field, and click Connect. Then don't forget to click Save (top-right) so the key is actually kept.
